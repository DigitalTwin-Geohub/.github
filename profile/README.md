Welcome to **GitHub@DigitalTwin-Geohub**! The [Digital Twin Geohub (DTG)](https://www.utwente.nl/en/digital-society/research/digitalisation/digital-twin-geohub/) at the [University of Twente](https://www.utwente.nl/en/) brings together researchers from the Faculty of Geo-Information Science and Earth Observation ([ITC](https://www.utwente.nl/en/itc/)) and the Faculty of Engineering Technology ([ET](https://www.utwente.nl/en/et/)) to work on global challenges such as climate change, biodiversity loss, pandemics, and pollution. We see a digital twin as a dynamic, live replica of the physical living environment, covering its past, current, and future states, that supports better decisions. This GitHub is where we develop and share the research code, data pipelines, tutorials, and teaching material behind that work. You are invited to use it, contribute to it, and build on it.

	<br>
	<ul>
	<p>
		<b>Vision.</b> We aim to establish digital-twin-based decision-making ecosystems in which academics, practitioners, and decision-makers collect, organise, and analyse heterogeneous data on one platform, working towards sustainable and resilient cities.
	</p>
	<p>
		<b>Mission.</b>
		<ul>
			<li>Use the hub for research and joint projects on digital twins for the planning, design, construction, and maintenance of sustainable and smart cities.</li>
			<li>Show how digital twins can be produced with cutting-edge technology and applied in practice.</li>
			<li>Exchange knowledge and experience through workshops and seminars.</li>
			<li>Be a strong national and international contributor to digital twin research.</li>
		</ul>
	</p>
	</ul>
</details>

### Our projects

Research code from the Digital Twin Geohub is published here, as well as in scientific repositories and on personal GitHub accounts. To find our work, have a look at:

- [GitHub@DigitalTwin-Geohub](https://github.com/orgs/DigitalTwin-Geohub/repositories) - All projects published on GitHub.com/DigitalTwin-Geohub.
- [GitHub topics](https://github.com/topics/digital-twin-geohub) - Projects tagged with [#digital-twin-geohub](https://github.com/topics/digital-twin-geohub).
- [News](https://www.utwente.nl/en/digital-society/research/digitalisation/digital-twin-geohub/news/) and [events](https://www.utwente.nl/en/digital-society/research/digitalisation/digital-twin-geohub/events/) - Updates, workshops, and seminars from the hub.

### Contributing

At the Digital Twin Geohub we value open science, so we welcome contributions to our projects. Are you interested in contributing? Great! Open an issue or a pull request in the repository you want to help with, and we will help you get started. New to open source? Learn more about [how to contribute](https://opensource.guide/).
